# Focus Bear Internship – Time Plan

## Overview

My goal is to consistently allocate enough time to complete the required internship work while maintaining a structured weekly schedule.

I will work remotely from my usual workspace and use Focus Bear to track my Focus Sessions whenever I am actively working on internship-related tasks.

My planned availability is **Monday to Friday, approximately 40 hours per week**.

---

| Day       | Working Hours | Planned Focus                                           |
| --------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Monday    | 09:00–17:00   | Onboarding tasks, research, QA learning, assigned tasks |
| Tuesday   | 09:00–17:00   | Assigned QA tasks, testing, documentation               |
| Wednesday | 09:00–17:00   | Testing, bug investigation, research                    |
| Thursday  | 09:00–17:00   | Assigned tasks, documentation, QA practice              |

# Daily Working Structure

A typical working day will be structured approximately as follows:

### Morning

**09:00–12:00**

* Review assigned tasks.
* Work on the highest-priority onboarding or QA task.
* Use Focus Bear Focus Sessions while actively working.

### Break

**12:00–13:00**

* Lunch and personal break.

### Afternoon

**13:00–17:00**

* Continue assigned tasks.
* Perform testing and research.
* Document findings.
* Review completed work and prepare the next steps.

The exact activities may change depending on the assigned internship tasks and team priorities.

---

# Time Tracking

I will use Focus Bear to track the time I spend actively working on Focus Bear internship tasks.

I will start a Focus Session when beginning focused internship work and stop it when the work session ends.

I will use the recorded Focus Sessions as evidence of the time spent on internship activities.

I will also make sure that the tracked time represents actual focused internship work rather than general computer usage.
