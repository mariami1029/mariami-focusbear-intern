# User Manual for Mariami Masurashvili

## My Role

* I am working as a Manual QA Intern at Focus Bear.
* My main responsibilities include learning the product, performing manual testing, identifying potential issues, documenting findings, and improving my QA skills.
* I work remotely and generally follow a Monday–Friday schedule.
* I am currently focused on developing my skills in manual testing, test case design, bug reporting, API testing, SQL, and test automation.

## How I Work Best

* I do my best work when I understand the goal of a task and what the expected outcome is.
* I like having clear instructions and enough context to understand why something needs to be done.
* I enjoy exploring a problem independently before asking for help.
* I prefer to break larger tasks into smaller, manageable steps.
* I work well when I have time to focus without unnecessary interruptions.
* I struggle when requirements are unclear and there is no way to understand what the expected behaviour should be.
* When requirements are unclear, I will first make reasonable assumptions and investigate the product before escalating questions.

## Communication Style

* I prefer communication to be clear, direct, and respectful.
* For simple questions or updates, I prefer short asynchronous messages.
* For more complex topics, I appreciate additional context or examples.
* Written instructions are particularly useful when a task has several steps or requirements.
* Discord or other team communication channels are suitable for day-to-day communication.
* If something is urgent, clearly indicate that it is urgent and explain what needs to be done and when it is needed.

## Collaboration & Teamwork

* I work best in a team where people communicate openly and are comfortable asking and answering questions.
* I appreciate teammates who share context instead of assuming that everyone already knows the background.
* I like collaborating on problems while still having enough independence to investigate and find solutions myself.
* My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is unclear communication or important information being left out.
* I also appreciate when responsibilities and expectations are clearly defined.
* If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am listening, processing the information, or thinking about what I want to contribute.

## Focus & Productivity

* I stay focused by breaking my work into clear tasks and working through them one at a time.
* I find structured to-do lists and clear priorities helpful.
* I prefer dedicated periods of uninterrupted work for tasks that require concentration.
* Things that distract me include unnecessary interruptions, unclear priorities, and constantly switching between unrelated tasks.
* The best way to support my productivity is to communicate priorities clearly and give me enough uninterrupted time to complete focused work.

## Feedback & Growth

* I like feedback to be clear, constructive, and specific.
* Examples are especially helpful because they show me exactly what I can improve.
* I prefer feedback to focus on the work or behaviour rather than the person.
* When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is to explain what went wrong, why it matters, and what I could do differently next time.
* I appreciate teammates who point out mistakes early rather than allowing a problem to grow.
* I also appreciate positive feedback when something is done well because it helps me understand what practices I should continue.
* I am comfortable receiving constructive criticism and see it as an important part of learning.

## Fun Facts & Quirks

* I tend to investigate problems thoroughly, especially when something does not behave as expected.

## My Commitment
