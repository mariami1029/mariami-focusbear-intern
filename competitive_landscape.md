# Competitive Landscape: Focus Bear vs Productivity & Focus Apps

## Overview

For this research, I compared Focus Bear with four productivity and focus applications: Freedom, Cold Turkey, Tiimo, and Routinery.

The goal was not to prove that Focus Bear is better than every competitor, but to understand where each product is strongest and where Focus Bear could improve.

---

## 1. Focus Bear

Focus Bear positions itself as an anti-distraction and anti-burnout application designed particularly with ADHD and neurodivergent users in mind.

Its main features include:

* Website and application blocking across Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android.
* Morning and evening routines that guide users through activities step by step.
* Habit stacking and custom routines.
* Break reminders designed to reduce prolonged periods of hyperfocus.
* Cross-device distraction blocking.
* Zen Mode for making a phone distraction-free during focus sessions.
* AI-based distraction detection.
* Customisable routines and blocked websites/apps.

A key difference is that Focus Bear does not focus only on blocking distractions. It combines **distraction blocking, routines, breaks, habits, and behavioural support** into one product.

---

# 2. Focus Bear vs Freedom

## What Freedom does well

Freedom is strongly focused on eliminating digital distractions.

Its main features include:

* Blocking websites.
* Blocking applications.
* Blocking the entire internet.
* Custom blocklists.
* Website exceptions / allow-only sessions.
* Scheduled and recurring focus sessions.
* Cross-device syncing.
* Locked Mode, which makes it harder to stop an active session.
* Focus sounds.
* Session history and annotations.

Freedom is particularly strong when the user's main problem is **digital distraction** and they want a highly configurable blocking system.

## How Focus Bear differs

Focus Bear goes beyond blocking.

For example, Focus Bear combines blocking with:

* Guided morning and evening routines.
* Habit stacking.
* Break reminders.
* Hyperfocus-related break support.
* Phone Zen Mode.
* AI distraction detection.

Therefore, I would describe Freedom as more **blocker/session-centric**, while Focus Bear is more **behaviour and routine-centric**.

## Why might I choose Focus Bear?

I would choose Focus Bear if my problem was not simply "I visit distracting websites too much", but rather:

> "I struggle to start my day, follow routines, take breaks, and stay focused consistently."

In that situation, having routines and focus support in the same application could be more useful than using a dedicated blocker.

## What does Freedom have that Focus Bear could improve on?

Freedom has a particularly strong system for creating custom blocklists and allow-only sessions. Users can block everything except the websites they actually need.

This could be an area where Focus Bear could provide more advanced control over exactly what is allowed during a focus session.

---

# 3. Focus Bear vs Cold Turkey

## What Cold Turkey does well

Cold Turkey is designed around strict distraction blocking.

Its features include:

* Website blocking.
* Application blocking.
* YouTube channel blocking.
* Keyword blocking.
* Entire-internet blocking.
* Computer locking.
* Weekly schedules.
* Time allowances and breaks.
* Locked blocks.
* Usage statistics.
* Uninstall prevention.
* Frozen Turkey, which can lock, log out, or shut down a computer.

Cold Turkey's biggest strength is **strict enforcement**.

It is deliberately designed to make bypassing a block difficult.

## How Focus Bear differs

Focus Bear also blocks websites and applications, but its approach is less focused on being an extremely strict blocker.

Instead, Focus Bear combines blocking with routines, breaks, habits, and other behavioural support.

This creates two different philosophies:

**Cold Turkey:**

> Make distractions difficult or impossible to access.

**Focus Bear:**

> Build an environment and routine that helps the user focus consistently.

## Why might I choose Focus Bear?

I would choose Focus Bear if I wanted more than a technical barrier.

For example, someone might need help with:

* Starting their morning routine.
* Remembering breaks.
* Transitioning between activities.
* Avoiding phone distractions.
* Maintaining productive habits.

Cold Turkey is very strong at preventing access to distractions, but Focus Bear provides a broader productivity and wellbeing system.

## What does Cold Turkey do better?

Cold Turkey has stronger enforcement mechanisms.

Its locking system can make it extremely difficult for users to disable active blocks or uninstall the software. This is a useful feature for users who know that they are likely to bypass their own restrictions.

Focus Bear could potentially improve by offering more granular and configurable "strict mode" options while still keeping its more supportive approach.

---

# 4. Focus Bear vs Tiimo

## What Tiimo does well

Tiimo is a visual planner designed for ADHD, Autism, and people who benefit from flexible structure.

Its features include:

* Visual planning.
* Calendar and task management.
* Routines.
* Reminders.
* Visual timers.
* Flexible scheduling.
* AI-assisted task breakdown.
* AI co-planning.
* The ability to rearrange plans when circumstances change.
* Support across iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, Android, and Web.

Tiimo's major strength is **visual planning and flexible organisation**.

It is particularly interesting because it does not simply tell users what tasks exist; it helps turn large tasks into smaller, more manageable steps.

## How Focus Bear differs

Focus Bear puts more emphasis on **focus execution and distraction prevention**.

Tiimo is stronger as a planning and scheduling tool, while Focus Bear is stronger as a tool for creating an environment in which the user can actually focus.

This means the products solve related but different problems.

## Why might I choose Focus Bear?

I would choose Focus Bear if my biggest problem was getting distracted while trying to work or study.

The combination of:

* Website/app blocking,
* phone blocking,
* routines,
* breaks,
* and distraction detection

could make Focus Bear more useful than a planner alone.

## What does Tiimo have that Focus Bear could improve on?

Tiimo has an AI co-planner that can turn natural-language input into structured plans and can break larger tasks into smaller steps.

This is one of the clearest areas where Focus Bear could improve.

### Potential improvement

Focus Bear could add an **AI task-planning assistant** that takes a large goal and converts it into a realistic sequence of smaller tasks, optionally scheduling them around the user's existing routine.

---

# 5. Focus Bear vs Routinery

## What Routinery does well

Routinery focuses heavily on running routines rather than simply displaying a checklist.

Its features include:

* Step-by-step routines.
* A timer for each routine step.
* Voice cues.
* Morning and evening routines.
* Streak tracking.
* Flexible routine scheduling.
* Location-based routine reminders.
* Checklists.

The main strength of Routinery is that it answers:

> "What should I be doing right now?"

rather than simply showing the user a list of tasks.

## How Focus Bear differs

There is significant overlap between the two products because Focus Bear also guides users through routines step by step.

However, Focus Bear combines routines with much stronger distraction-prevention functionality.

For example, Focus Bear can connect routines with:

* Website/app blocking.
* Phone Zen Mode.
* Break management.
* Focus sessions.
* AI distraction detection.

Routinery is therefore more specialised around **routine execution**, while Focus Bear attempts to combine routine execution with **focus and distraction management**.

## Why might I choose Focus Bear?

I would choose Focus Bear if I wanted my routine and focus environment to be connected.

For example:

1. Start a focus session.
2. Block distracting websites.
3. Put the phone into Zen Mode.
4. Work for a period of time.
5. Receive a break reminder.
6. Continue with the next part of the routine.

This creates a more complete workflow than a standalone routine timer.

## What does Routinery do particularly well?

Routinery's step-by-step timer experience is very focused and simple.

This suggests that Focus Bear should make sure its routine experience remains extremely clear and low-friction rather than becoming overloaded with too many productivity features.

# 6. Overall Comparison

| Product         | Strongest area                            | Main approach                  |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Focus Bear**  | Focus + routines + distraction prevention | Behavioural support            |
| **Freedom**     | Cross-device blocking                     | Digital distraction prevention |
| **Cold Turkey** | Strict blocking and enforcement           | Prevent access to distractions |
| **Tiimo**       | Visual planning and flexible scheduling   | Planning and organisation      |
| **Routinery**   | Guided routines and timers                | Routine execution              |

---

# 7. What Makes Focus Bear Different?

The biggest differentiator is the combination of multiple systems in one application.

Focus Bear combines:

**Distraction blocking + routines + habits + breaks + phone control + focus sessions + distraction detection**

rather than concentrating on only one of these areas.

The product is also explicitly designed around the needs of neurodivergent users. Focus Bear describes itself as being built by a neurodivergent team based on lived experience and feedback from neurodivergent users.

This gives the product a more specific target audience than a generic productivity application.

---

# 8. Why Would I Choose Focus Bear?

If I were choosing between these applications, I would choose Focus Bear if I wanted an all-in-one system rather than a single-purpose productivity tool.

For example:

* If I only wanted website blocking → Freedom or Cold Turkey might be better.
* If I primarily wanted visual planning → Tiimo might be better.
* If I mainly wanted guided timed routines → Routinery might be better.
* If I wanted a combination of routines, distraction blocking, phone control, breaks and focus support → Focus Bear would be a strong choice.

This is important because Focus Bear does not necessarily need to beat every competitor at their strongest individual feature.

Its competitive advantage can instead be the **combination of features and the specific experience created by putting them together**.

---

# 9. One Feature I Think Focus Bear Could Add

## AI-powered task planning

One feature I would consider adding is an AI planning assistant similar to Tiimo's AI planning functionality.

A user could enter:

> "I need to prepare my university presentation by Friday."

The system could turn this into smaller steps such as:

1. Define the presentation topic.
2. Research the topic.
3. Create an outline.
4. Prepare slides.
5. Review the slides.
6. Practice the presentation.

The user could then choose which steps should be scheduled into their routine.

This would complement Focus Bear's existing focus and distraction-prevention features instead of replacing them.

---

# 10. Recommended Improvement

Based on this research, my main recommendation would be:

## Add stronger planning and task-decomposition capabilities.

Focus Bear is already strong at helping users **execute** routines and stay focused.

The next logical step would be helping users decide **what to do and how to break it down**.

This would create a more complete workflow:

**Plan → Break down → Schedule → Focus → Take breaks → Complete → Review**

That would allow Focus Bear to compete not only as a distraction blocker, but as a more complete productivity system for users who struggle with planning, task initiation, and maintaining focus.

---

# Conclusion

Focus Bear does not need to be better than Freedom, Cold Turkey, Tiimo, or Routinery in every category.

Each competitor has a clear strength:

* Freedom excels at flexible cross-device blocking.
* Cold Turkey excels at strict enforcement.
* Tiimo excels at visual planning and flexible task organisation.
* Routinery excels at guided, timed routines.

Focus Bear's strongest opportunity is to combine its existing strengths into a coherent experience specifically designed around focus, routines, and neurodivergent-friendly productivity.

The most valuable improvement I identified is stronger task planning and AI-assisted task decomposition. This could complement Focus Bear's existing focus tools and help users move from knowing what they need to accomplish to actually getting started.
